Prototype · wave-in

A staggered rise-and-fade — the same load animation as the “Domis” title on hridaew.com/domis, rebuilt as pure CSS (no GSAP, no libraries). Each letter starts low and transparent and lifts into place a beat after the one before it. Shown here on the real hero, at real scale.

Product Designer with 6+ years of experience designing end-to-end

How it works: JS splits each heading into <span class="ch"> letters and sets --i (the letter’s index) on each. CSS turns that into a per-letter animation-delay, so one keyframe produces the whole wave. The subtitle simply starts its count later so it trails the name. Swap the values with the sliders, then I’ll bake the ones you like into index.html.
